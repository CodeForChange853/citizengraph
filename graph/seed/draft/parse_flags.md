# Parse flags

Generated by `python -m citizengraph.parsing`. These are places where the sheet is blank, merged, or not a number. Nothing was guessed: values are left null or raw. Fold anything that needs the LGU into `docs/charter_data.md` section 5.

| Flag | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `fee_no_currency_sign` | 1 | Step fee is a bare number with no peso sign; read as an amount. |
| `fee_text_only` | 1 | Step fee is text with no amount (e.g. 'As determined by the CSWMO'). |
| `merged_cell_shared` | 23 | Step's fee/time cell is merged with a step above; value counted once. |
| `no_requirements_listed` | 13 | The checklist says N/A; stored as no requirements. |
| `orphan_row_glued` | 14 | A row with no fee or time continues the step above (wrapped text); appended to it. |
| `person_missing` | 4 | Step has no person responsible. |
| `possible_split_step` | 1 | A step with no fee or time is followed by a row with no label or person; possibly one step split over two rows. Kept as two. |
| `requirement_fragment_glued` | 2 | A labelled requirement wrapped onto the next row; the rows were joined. |
| `time_ambiguous` | 1 | Step time is a decimal such as '1.15 min'; kept raw, not converted. |
| `time_not_stated` | 16 | Step has no processing time ('- - -' or blank). |
| `total_time_typo_normalized` | 1 | Stated total time contains a known unit typo that was read as its unit (e.g. 'dsys' as days). |
| `where_to_secure_missing` | 7 | Requirement has no 'where to secure'; stored as null. |
| `where_to_secure_na` | 1 | 'Where to secure' says N/A; stored as null. |
| `where_to_secure_shared` | 6 | 'Where to secure' is a merged cell shared with another row; value copied and flagged. |
| `who_may_avail_blank` | 4 | Who may avail is blank in the sheet; stored as null. |
